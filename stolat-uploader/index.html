StoLat Uploader

StoLat Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel StoLatStoLat to publish personalised Polish birthday songs ("Sto lat").

It runs only on the channel owner's own computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and post comments on the channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.
