StoLat Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

StoLat Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel StoLatStoLat. It is used only by that owner, on their own computer, to manage their own channel.

What data the tool accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel, edit their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and settings, add them to the channel's playlists, read the view counts of the channel's own videos, and post and read comments on the channel's own videos. It reads basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm it is connected to the right channel. It does not access any other user's account.

How data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. To keep track of its own production, the tool records the IDs, titles, publishing status and view counts of the channel's own videos in the owner's private database, on a server rented and controlled by the owner. This database is not public and is not shared with anyone. The tool has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of viewers.

Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. StoLat Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections or through Google security settings, which also invalidates the stored token. The stored records can be deleted on request to the contact address below.
